Who We Are

About OCSF

Orphanage Care and Support Foundation is dedicated to providing holistic support for vulnerable children, youth, and women in impoverished communities. With a deep commitment to empowerment and transformation, we strive to break the cycle of poverty and provide hope for a brighter future.

Our Vision

To create a world where every orphaned child and woman in need experiences love, stability, and the opportunity to reach their full potential, fostering a future generation that thrives and contributes meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

To empower orphaned and vulnerable children and women in need through comprehensive care, quality education, and nurturing environments, ensuring they grow into self-reliant, confident, and compassionate individuals capable of transforming their communities.

What We Offer

Our Services

At OCSF, we offer a range of programs and services aimed at addressing the diverse needs of those we serve — from basic necessities to education and healthcare.

Widow & Orphan Support

Essential resources, emotional counseling, healthcare, and financial assistance for widows and orphans to foster stability and self-sufficiency.

Health Education Workshop

Vital knowledge on maternal healthcare, hygiene, nutrition, and disease prevention to promote healthier lifestyles and overall well-being.

Free Education for Children

Free tuition, supplies, and academic support for children in need, ensuring access to quality educational opportunities regardless of circumstance.

Digital Employability Skills

Comprehensive digital training programs equipping women, girls, youth at risk, and special needs persons with skills to thrive in a technology-driven world.

Free Medical Care

Free medical check-ups, treatments, medications, and surgeries for vulnerable individuals in underserved communities through regular outreach programs.

Women Empowerment

Skills acquisition, business startup support, and trauma care empowering women to achieve financial independence and build better lives for their families.
