At Orphanage Care and Support Foundation, we are dedicated to breaking the cycle of poverty and providing hope for a brighter future for vulnerable children, youth, and women in poor communities. If you are passionate about making a difference and want to be part of a team that creates lasting impact, we invite you to explore career opportunities with us.

Why OCSF

Why Work With Us?

When you work with OCSF, you are part of something much larger than a job. You become a catalyst for transformational change in the lives of those who need it most.

Meaningful Impact

Every day you come to work, you are directly contributing to the upliftment of vulnerable children, youth, and women in Nigerian communities.

Growth & Learning

We invest in our team members' professional development with training, mentorship, and opportunities to grow within the organization.

Collaborative Culture

Join a team of passionate, dedicated individuals who share a common vision and support each other in achieving it.
